About us

People who happen to be very good with animals

World Pet Care started as a single neighbourhood clinic with one simple idea: treat every animal the way you'd want your own pet treated. Today that same philosophy guides every check-up, phone call, and late-night emergency we handle — calm hands, honest advice, and no rushing you out the door.
